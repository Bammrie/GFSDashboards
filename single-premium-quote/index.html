Prototype Purpose

This prototype is designed to demonstrate the loan officer quote flow and calculation structure. The final production calculator should use the carrier-approved single premium formula, discount/annuity factor, rate tables, rounding rules, and eligibility rules.

Supported Calculation Modes

A. Carrier Formula Placeholder

This mode is reserved for the exact CSO/carrier formula once provided. The app is structured so the approved formula can replace the demo formula without changing the user interface.

B. Annuity Factor Demo

AF = (1 - (1 + i)^(-n)) / i
  • i = monthly interest rate
  • n = loan term in months

The annuity factor is used as a present value factor for a stream of monthly balances/payments. It is included because single premium credit insurance on reducing-balance loans may require discounting or factoring instead of a simple flat term multiplier.

C. Flat Rate Demo

Premium = Loan Amount / 1000 × Rate × Term

This is included only for comparison and quick testing. It should not be assumed to be the final carrier-approved single premium method.
