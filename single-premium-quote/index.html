Focused explanation of the current monthly gross-pay prototype math.

Current Prototype Scope

This prototype is currently configured for monthly loan payments, gross pay basis, 7-day retro Disability, and Missouri/Arkansas state selection based on carrier guidance.

Gross Pay Basis

Gross Pay generally means the calculation may be tied to the scheduled payment stream rather than only the declining net principal balance. The exact CSO definition of gross pay must be confirmed before production.

Estimated Monthly Payment x Number of Monthly Payments = Gross Pay Exposure
Gross Pay Exposure / 1,000 x Demo Rate = Prototype Single Premium

Monthly Payments Only

Only monthly loan payments are enabled in this prototype. Annual, quarterly, semi-monthly, biweekly, and weekly frequencies are intentionally excluded for now.

7-Day Retro Disability

Credit Disability is currently locked to 7-Day Retro based on carrier guidance. Other waiting periods are excluded from this prototype.

Carrier Formula Placeholder

Once CSO provides Missouri and Arkansas rate information, exact formula rules, factor tables, and test cases, the demo calculation can be replaced inside the quote engine without changing the loan officer interface.
